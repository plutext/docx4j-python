A tracked `insert_text` is 45 us against 18 us untracked, the id scan is 7.4 ms once per package, and `accept_all()` over 3,900 changes is 30 ms. Phase E is custom XML, XML mapping and the typed content controls over **lxml's own XPath 1.0**, so there is no engine to build and nothing asynchronous: `pkg.custom_xml_parts` with `get_item` brace- and case-insensitive, `add(xml)` following docx4j's `addPropertiesPart` (the properties part beside it, a brace-wrapped upper-case `ds:itemID` from the package's seeded generator, the relationship from the **main document part**) and `delete()` unlinking the mappings it can reach; the DOM-shaped `CustomXmlNode` with Word's canonical positional `xpath`; `XmlMapping` reading `w:dataBinding` **and** `w15:dataBinding` and resolving an XPath before it writes one; the seven typed kinds; `insert_content_control` on a body, a paragraph and a range; `apply_bindings()` / `update_from_content_controls()` with docx4j's shapes and section 4's four departures (containers never bound, pictures and rich text skipped, dates formatted and checkboxes and lists round-tripped by value, the content into the first paragraph); and `describe()` / `fill()`, the library half of docx4j-mcp's two template tools. The engine change it needed is that **parsing an lxml part no longer modifies it**: `DefaultXmlPart` tells "parsed for reading" from "adopted", so a document whose custom XML is read end to end saves byte for byte. `apply_bindings` over the invoice's 20 bindings is 1.8 ms, `describe()` 1.0 ms and 6.5 KB of JSON, and importing `docx4j_py` is unchanged because the package is imported on first use. Every custom XML mutation records a `ChangeReport` like every other content-API call, with one report for a whole `fill()` (section 17.9). The Word check of artefact 9 then found the one thing section 17 had wrong about Word and produced two follow-ups the same day: **17.10**, Word expands a bound repeating section to its node set when it opens the document (so the engine is right not to, and `fill()` now takes a **list** for a repeat --- one data node per entry, cloned from the template node, with `describe().repeats` naming the fields an entry takes); and **17.11**, `pkg.compatibility_mode`, an `int` over `w:compatSetting compatibilityMode` read with lxml and cached (12 when the document declares nothing, which is what Word assumes), with a `ChangeReport.warnings` line wherever a verb writes a `w15` feature into a document below mode 15 and no silent change of the mode. 1,232 tests (74 of them Phase E's), and with Phase E **all 200 non-extension members of `tests/office_js_subset.json` are implemented**. Phase H is lists --- `List` and `ListItem` in Office JS's vocabulary, `start_new_list()` / `attach_to_list` / `detach_from_list`, the three level setters, and under them the numbering **`Emulator`** CR-002 section 6.2 had promised for its Phase B, measured against the labels Word painted in six probe documents (CR-002 section 12.11). Phase I is `to_api_script`: the content-API calls that would produce what you show it, as Python that **executes as it stands**, with `body.insert_xml(...)` of the marshalled fragment for what the verbs cannot express, and an executed round trip in the tests that compares every block the verbs claimed to the source canonically. Phase J is the **python-docx facade** (`docx4j_py.docx`): a structural subset of python-docx's public API in a namespace of its own, with python-docx's semantics where they differ from Office JS's --- tri-state `Font` booleans, EMU `Length` units, a style object with `.name`, the layout-grid cell walk that honours `w:gridSpan` and `w:vMerge` --- over the content API and, where python-docx has a member it does not, over the typed tree inside a `recording` block, so a facade call leaves a `ChangeReport` like any other. Its promise is `tests/python_docx_subset.json`, derived by introspecting an installed python-docx 1.2.0: 33 interfaces, 303 members, **295 implemented** and eight refused with a reason each; and a parity test runs one ordinary python-docx script through both libraries and reads both files back **with python-docx**, which passes on every one of its twelve comparisons. **Every phase of CR-003 is now implemented** (sections 10 to 20); 1,530 tests; what the CR leaves open is section 20.8 |
